# Aurora Viewer (Otoa's Mod)

This is not the original Aurora Viewer. The original project is at https://github.com/Aurora-Viewer/Aurora-Viewer

This is my personal clone of it with my own patches, shared only so my patched source can be inspected. It is not for others to use: no builds, no support, and it is not part of the Aurora Viewer project.

## Version

This clone is caught up with the main branch of the original, 11 commits after `v0.9.14-beta.20261126.1` (commit `3ed25144a`).

## Patches used

Each one is a file in scripts/patches/, applied at build time.

- 10-telemetry: no update checks, login news, crash or bug reports, or GIF service counters. The statistics report to Linden Lab stays on, as its viewer policy requires.
- 20-spacemouse: SpaceMouse works on Linux without a udev rule.
- 25-flycam-keys: the keyboard still moves the avatar while the joystick flycam is on.
- 27-script-dialog: a setting for where script dialogs appear.
- 28-credentials: the saved login is sealed with a local tool instead of the desktop keyring.
- 30-audit: small security fixes from an audit (grid links, media filter, link previews, plugin memory).
- 35-sl-grids-only: logs in to the two Second Life grids only.
- 36-channel-name: logs in as "Aurora Viewer (Otoa's Mod)", never as the original viewer.
- 40-clippy: the place for fixes found by the Clippy lint check. It is empty, because the check found nothing to fix.
- 45-moap-slurl: secondlife:// links clicked on in-world web pages follow the Legacy Viewer's safety rules for untrusted pages.
- 50-aurora-shader: a host for user post-processing effects; effects can also read the depth of transparent faces (contract v2).
- 51-aurora-shader-prefs: a Preferences page and a toolbar button for those effects; the page has one tab per effect, dragged to reorder.
- 52-shader-photo-look: those effects as one look in the photo look stack.
- 55-bandwidth-cap: the bandwidth setting goes up to 30,000 kb/s.
- 65-chat-import: the chat import window follows the chat logging settings.
- 76-cef-site-profiles: Chromium keeps cookies only for listed sites.
- 77-trust-engine: TRust as a second web engine beside Chromium. Its host program is not in this repository.
- 78-url-log: an optional local log of the web addresses of media and chat, for a separate checking program; off by default.
- 80-window-shadow: a window shadow strength slider.
- 82-media-off: with media turned off, nothing about media leaves the viewer.
- 83-channel-switches: a sound channel can be turned off completely, not only muted.
- 84-media-filter-switches: two more media filter switches, "Don't report blocked" and "Don't ask".
- 85-media-filter-guard: a check that the media filter stays on for all media; it changes nothing today.
- 86-minimize-stall: a minimized window is no longer reported as a stalled graphics card.
- 87-region-crossing-origin: no stall at region crossings.
- 89-draw-distance-1024: the photo boost raises the draw distance to 1,024 m (upstream now has the 1,024 m range itself).
- 90-scene-impostors: optional far-field impostors, off by default.
- 91-caustics-water-texture: optional underwater caustics from the water's own texture, off by default; during an environment change they cross-fade with the water, and to or from water without a texture they dim through the middle of the change instead of switching pattern.
- 92-anim-restart-legacy: an animation signalled again keeps playing instead of restarting.
- 93-ao-over-scripted-overrides: the animation overrider wins over scripted overrides.
- 94-ao-sit-cover-order: the overrider's sit animation shows over its cover pose.
- 95-ao-repair-any-loop: the overrider also repairs lost one-frame poses.
- 96-follow-sim-list: with "Synchronize" on, animations follow the simulator's list exactly.
- 97-new-object-textures: default texture, normal map and specular map for new objects.
- 98-animation-explorer-played: an accurate "Played" column in the Animation explorer.
- 99-hover-leave: the world hover stops when the cursor leaves the window.
- 100-feet-height: a "Feet height" slider from -1 to +1 m, local only.
- 101-chat-scroll-speed: chat lines slide up instead of jumping.
- 102-year-first-dates: dates read year, month, day.
- 103-inventory-display: two inventory display switches: hide empty system folders, and bold folders that hold worn items.
- 104-ao-list-height: the animation list of the AO window grows with the window.
- 105-weather-size: snowflakes can be up to three times their usual size, and a new "Drop size" slider sizes the raindrops the same way.
- 105-weather-top-end: the top quarter of the weather's Amount slider falls harder: a downpour or a blizzard draws up to four times the drops or flakes, their splashes and the distant rain's streaks, and the near ones stand out more (one and a half times their opacity); below three quarters nothing changes.
- 106-director-readd: in the Director, a keyframe added right after deleting the first or the last one goes back where the deleted one was.
- 109-ocean-swell: optional long rolling swells on open water (Ocean Swell Down and Up: how far their troughs fall and their crests rise, 0 to 10 m each), calmer water near land and static objects (Calm distance and Calm at the edge, for the swell and the geometric waves alike), reflections bent by the water's own normal instead of the water mesh's grid, and physical or moving keyframed objects, swimmers and the avatars sitting on them optionally riding the swell; during an environment change to water whose big wave points another way, the old swell fades out where it is while the new one fades in along its own direction, none turning or sliding; all on this screen only, in the enhanced water's gear; the swell off by default.
- 111-swim-feet: Aurora foot placement rests while your own avatar swims and comes back by itself when the swim ends.
- 112-voice-neighbours: nearby voice is heard across region borders: on the region-wide channel, a connection to each neighbouring region within reach, the microphone sent only to your own region's.
- 113-director-live-frames: a Director Live film fetches several frames from the graphics card at once (a "Frames fetched at once" setting, 1 to 10, default 3; 1 is the original one at a time) and ignores the frame-rate limits while it records, for a smoother film; frames still on their way when it stops are kept.
- 114-sky-rays: optional volumetric light rays from the sun or the moon, drawn through the shadows; off by default. The code is ported from the commit https://github.com/rickrizzlers/Aurora-Sun-Moon-Preview/commit/c5d71a4a61c9ef7e6f280dc2059ca87703e755b1
- 115-grid-features-opt-in: two upstream features that talk to the grid on their own are off by default: the direct-message history fetched from the grid at login, and Game Controls input sent to the region; both stay switchable in Preferences where upstream puts them.
- 116-hide-owner-objects: "Hide all objects of <owner>" in the object menu hides every rezzed object of that resident (or of the group of a deeded object), listed and undone in the Asset blacklist window; the owner is asked of the region only while such a rule exists, a few at a time. Also: blocking an object stops its particles, and blocking the owner of a group-owned object whose group name is unknown says so instead of doing nothing.
- 117-water-height-offsets: "Flotation height" of swimming from -1 to +1 m, a "Surface swimming height" under it (-1 to +1 m added to the flotation height while swimming along the surface, eased in and out, so the floating and swimming animations each sit right), and a "Water walking height" beside them (-1 to +1 m, below 0 wading); all are real positions the region and others see, sent in the existing bridge command (the worn script is unchanged); while water walking, a solid object's top just under the water (between the held feet and a little above the surface) carries the avatar, which stands and walks on it and is caught again over open water.
- 118-radio-strict-certificates: an `https` parcel radio whose certificate fails verification (expired, self-signed, untrusted issuer, another name) is refused again instead of played; the radio button's red sign, its tooltip and one nearby chat line per station say why. No setting; a guard fails the patch run if a lenient verifier comes back in the audio code.
- 119-mouselook-smoothing: a "Mouselook smoothing" choice in Preferences (Off by default; Frame average, Exponential, One Euro, Damped spring, Leading cursor) with one Strength slider; the avatar turns with the smoothed view, so others see what you see, and nothing sent changes rate; in Leading cursor mode the crosshair leads and clicks go where it is.
- 120-water-normal-shape: the small waves' rise and fall optionally shaped by the environment's own water normal map (Wave shape from the normal map, 0 to 1: 0 is today's geometric waves, 1 the normal map alone; Normal map scale, x1 to x100: the pattern enlarged, as much taller as wider), its slopes integrated into a height map on the thread pool when the map changes; shading, camera waterline, contacts and droplets follow the same shape, the ocean swell stays on top; on this screen only, in the enhanced water's gear; off by default.
- 121-occlusion-streaming: objects that stay hidden behind others (2 s, or at once if never seen) load their textures as if out of view and keep the mesh detail level they show, then take their full levels in the frame they show again; the GPU occlusion test reads back two bits per object a few frames late, never waited for; avatars, what they wear, selected objects and alpha-tested shadow casters are left alone; "Lighter loading behind walls" behind the Occlusion cog (on by default, "Hidden for" 0.5 to 10 s); only HTTP asset requests change.
- 122-mirror-pass: the planar mirror's pass costs less with no change to a flat mirror on the screen: what lies behind the mirror is dropped before it is shaded or culled whole, the pass is left out when the volume that can show it is off the screen or under 2 pixels across (the probes show, as with no mirror) and draws nothing when the main view's depth hides that volume, and it draws only that volume's screen rect (a glossy surface in the volume that is not parallel to the mirror fades to the probes for the rays that leave it, as for those that leave the screen); no setting; `perf summary` tells what became of the mirror each second.
- 123-sky-mode-blend: an environment change between a modern sky (with a reflection probe ambiance) and a classic one, or a day cycle between such keyframes, moves the presentation with its progress instead of switching it in one frame at its start or end: the brightness scale (geometrically), the tone curve's mix, the legacy gamma, the automatic exposure's range and its dark scene term, and the light and hemisphere colours follow the modern share; the surface shaders' lighting model, which cannot be mixed, takes the modern one only at a share of 1, where the exposure has its whole range, and two gains on the classic lighting's sun and ambient light ((modern level / classic level) ^ share, exactly 1 off such a change) carry a lit surface's level across that switch (a face turned up or to the light steps by 0.03 to 0.3 stop there, simulated, where it stepped by 0.6 to 1.1; shaded sides, walls, colour and reflections still differ); skies of one kind are unchanged to the bit.
- 126-demo-sky-change: a demo test variable, `AURORA_DEMO_SKY_CHANGE=<sky A>;<sky B>[;<seconds>[;<frame>]]`: the demo picks sky A at once, then sky B over the given seconds, through the environment selector as a sky picked by hand is, to capture a change between two given skies (kind, gamma, sun and moon, sunlight and ambient, haze and its multipliers, cloud shadow, moon brightness) on the GPU; the viewer is unchanged without it.
- 127-probe-change-pacing: during an environment change the reflection probes follow the change smoothly: the sky's probe (the one that lights and reflects what no local probe covers) is captured again after every two local probe captures instead of every 2 s, and each local probe cross-fades over half a second from its previous cube into its new capture instead of switching in one frame, the two farthest probes giving their cube slots up while the change runs (they fade out, and back in after it) so that a built-up region where every slot is taken has room for the cross-fades; the face budget is the same (no extra cost), and every probe is captured once more when the change ends; without a change nothing differs. Test variables `AURORA_PROBES=<level>[,<count>[,<range>]]`, `AURORA_MIRRORS`, `AURORA_WATER_REFLECTIONS`, and `AURORA_PROBE_CHANGE_PACING=0` for the probes as before.
- 128-constellations-and-planets: constellations over the real night sky: as a figure comes into view at night it draws itself like a pen stroke (its stick lines, then its engraving, then its name), and fades out when it leaves the view or the sky grows light, never in one frame; names lie level with the horizon (near the zenith they turn with the view instead) and the figures turn exactly with the stars. Its own window, built like the Aurora Weather window (World > Environment > Constellations, a toolbar button that opens and closes it, and a button in Preferences > Just for fun; all held by RLVa's environment lock like the weather): Show constellations (on), the engraved figures' opacity (1) and a check mark per art set of the data, Lines and their width (x1), Names, Obsolete figures (off), drawing time (4 s), fade-out time (3 s, up to 10), field around the view (50 degrees), sun under the horizon (8 degrees), names' size (16 pt), and the effects: drawing brightness (x1), a soft pulse as each part finishes appearing (on), a pastel colour per constellation (off), sparkles at the lines' pen tips with their size, shape and turns (off). "See licences and attribution" lists the data's sources from its `credits.json` files. Code only: the lines, names, drawings and credits are data the user installs in `constellations/` of the configuration folder (or `AURORA_CONSTELLATION_DIR`), read on demand as untrusted input with size limits; without it nothing is drawn and the window names the folder. Only the main view's sky draws them (no mirror, water or probe), nothing runs while it is off, and nothing is sent or written to the grid. Test variables `AURORA_DEMO_CONSTELLATIONS`, `AURORA_DEMO_CONSTELLATIONS_WINDOW=1`, `AURORA_DEMO_CONSTELLATION_CREDITS`. The planets in the real night sky: Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and faint Uranus where they really are among the stars, drawn as steady points, with their names from the constellation data folder, and two display-only switches for the Sun and the Moon (drawn at their real positions, or hidden); the region's light does not change. The constellation sets of other sky cultures beside the western one, read from the same folder: each brings its own figures, lines, drawings and names per language, and several can be ticked at once in the window. The western figures' names can come from the data folder's names file in any interface language, before the built-in English, French and Japanese names; the old figures that are no longer counted are named the same way. The window, its Preferences rows and the planets' rows are also in the twelve community languages (draft translations, not proofread by a native speaker). A Japanese name that ends with its kana reading in brackets shows the reading over its kanji, in letters half the name's size, as a book prints a reading over a word; every other name is drawn as before.
- 998-env-texture-fade: during an environment change (another environment, a parcel's, an altitude zone's, a local one) the clouds, sun, moon, rainbow, halo and water cross-fade over the change's own time, each of the two layers drawn exactly as its own sky or water draws it (the clouds with their own texture, offsets, size, warp and scroll speed, each scrolling from its own position; the water with its own normal map, wave directions, wave speeds and normal scale, and the ocean swell of patch 109 along its own direction), so a change of any of them dissolves instead of sliding, zooming or speeding up, and a texture still loading joins without a pop (fading in even after the change's time); a day cycle's keyframes still blend as before (no fade), and the extra reads happen only while a change lasts.
- 999-eased-environment-change: an environment change eases in and out (its progress follows a cosine, (1 - cos(pi p)) / 2, over exactly its own time; 0 s stays instant) where the Legacy Viewer's is linear, with everything it drives taking that one progress (values, kind shares, texture shares); a day cycle is not eased.
- 999-light-handover: during an environment change the sky's light (the sun, the moon or none) hands over from the source sky's to the target's at the change's own progress instead of switching in one frame where the blended sun or moon crosses the horizon: each light's share is lerped and every value the Legacy choice switches (the sky's light, the glow, the objects' and the haze's light, the rainbow and the halo) is their mix, the light's direction turns from one sky's light to the other's along the shorter great circle (a body's light from below the horizon is none, so the shadows and traced lights switch at no strength), and the sky's and clouds' glow, the sky rays and the water caustics follow the shares; still skies (twilight ones included) and day cycles are unchanged to the bit. Test variable `AURORA_LIGHT_HANDOVER=0` for the change as before. It replaces the former 124-sun-moon-handover.
- 999-performance: the same viewer, faster, applied after all the others: the feet's support traces and the water walking probe share one search of the nearby objects per avatar a frame, staged texture uploads get room of their own (the same textures, sooner), the calm water, swell and water shape maps and three other background jobs leave the frame's threads, the complexity rules' lists are kept between frames, the occlusion verdicts are read from their set bits only, the sky rays march with one multiply-add a sample, a few per-frame allocations go, and three chatty log lines are summed up; nothing drawn, played or sent changes.
